## Express permitted-use exceptions

PolyForm Noncommercial expressly permits use by charitable organizations,
educational institutions, public research organizations, public safety or health
organizations, environmental protection organizations, and government
institutions regardless of their funding source or resulting obligations.
These users do not automatically require a commercial license. Personal uses
specified in the license and other noncommercial purposes are also permitted.
A paid supplier must assess its own activities under the license; an eligible
customer does not automatically make all supplier activities permitted.

## Separately licensed SDKs and dependencies

The mirrors in `packages/python-sdk/` and `packages/typescript-sdk/` keep their
own Apache 2.0 licenses. Their upstream repositories are the source of truth.
They remain available for commercial integration; their licenses do not grant
commercial rights to newly restricted platform changes. Third-party dependencies
and their copyright, license and notice requirements are unaffected.

## Before releasing the transition

The owner must confirm copyright ownership or written permission for material
being offered under the new license, review the organization exceptions above,
and approve the commercial license process. Do not describe this change as a
retroactive prohibition on use of the Apache edition.
